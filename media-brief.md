## Supported finding

The reviewed material treats permission, contract scope, compensation, asset control, disclosure, provenance, lifecycle controls and remedies as different parts of the operating environment. No source in this bounded dataset addresses all eight controls.

## Required scope note

This is a selective review of 14 primary sources. It is not a global law census, legal advice, a market-size study or evidence about SocialGravity transactions. Authority class and status remain separate in the released data.
